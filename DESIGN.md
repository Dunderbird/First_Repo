## Brand & Style
The design narrative channels an unapologetic, time-tested North Jersey Italian-American heritage. It evokes the storied, no-nonsense warmth of a neighborhood institution—interlocking the familiar comfort of red-and-white checkered tablecloth dining with the sharp swagger of Satriale’s pork store and classic corner slice shops. 

The aesthetic style merges **Tactile Heritage** with **High-Contrast Editorial Precision**. It avoids ironic kitsch in favor of authentic family pride, culinary confidence, and neighborhood authority. Physical cues are translated through warm parchment paper grounds, robust structural framing, crisp vintage typography, and purposeful, tactile interaction states. The emotional response is instantly crave-inducing, deeply rooted, hospitable, and briskly efficient—commanding respect while inviting you to pull up a vinyl booth and break bread.

## Colors
The palette is deeply rooted in heritage Italian-American gastronomy:
- **Primary (`#991B1B` / Marinara Red):** The foundational brand color representing slow-simmered Sunday gravy. Used for primary CTAs, active selections, section headers, and key badge accents. An active hover state scales into vibrant crushed tomato red (`#B91C1C`).
- **Secondary (`#166534` / Basil Olive):** The mark of quality and freshness. Reserved for secondary actions, vegetarian/specialty callouts, confirmed badge statuses, and heritage stamp details.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` / Baked Mozzarella Gold):** The rich golden-crust accent. Deployed strategically for star ratings, specialty house ribbons, pricing highlights, and toast notifications.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FFFDF9` to `#FDF8F0` / Warm Parchment):** The foundation of the light theme, preventing sterile digital whites and evoking the tactile feel of clean parchment bakery paper and deli butcher wrap.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1F1612` / Dark Espresso):** High-contrast text ink with a rich roasted undertone rather than pure digital black, enhancing typographic warmth and readability.
- **Subtle Linework (`#E7DEC8` / Crust Rim Border):** Used for tactile component borders, tab dividers, and table separation, echoing aged flour dust and baker boards.

## Typography
Typographic scale pairs the sturdy, classic American storefront presence of **Domine** with the robust, neutral legibility of **Work Sans**.

- **Domine (Headlines & Menu Item Titles):** Heavy serifs with bold bracketed terminals convey classic woodblock and hand-painted pizzeria signboards. Headings are confident, grounded, and unmistakably old-school without feeling fragile or fussy.
- **Work Sans (Body, Microcopy, & Operational UI):** Provides maximum legibility under rapid glance conditions, whether scanning ingredients, customizing toppings, or reviewing order totals.
- **Labeling:** All interactive badges, dietary indicators, size pickers, and category tickers utilize uppercase tracking via `label-sm` and `label-md` to mirror stamped deli price cards and kitchen ticket printers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined, structured 12-column grid system configured for frictionless ordering:
- **Canvas & Section Margins:** Fluid margins ranging from 16px (`1rem`) on handheld mobile to 48px (`3rem`) on wide screens, guaranteeing comfortable edge isolation against the rich parchment backdrop.
- **Menu & Order Split Screen:** On desktop viewports (1024px and above), the screen splits into an 8-column scrollable culinary showcase (menu categories, customizable menu cards, and heritage specials) paired with a persistent 4-column sticky sidebar for the live order docket.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Single-column layout with category jump chips anchored beneath a compact banner, concluding with a persistent sticky bottom bar showing the total price and an expandable checkout drawer.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Maintained through multiples of 4px and 8px, with structured groupings (`space-sm` for price tags and toppings, `space-lg` between section headers and card clusters).

## Elevation & Depth
The system achieves depth through **Tactile Offset Framing** and **Warm Ambient Casts**, eschewing cold digital drop shadows:
- **Tier 0 (Surface Base):** The textured parchment ground (`#FFFDF9`) acts as the flat baseline.
- **Tier 1 (Menu Cards & Plates):** Clean butcher-paper white (`#FFFFFF`) layered atop the background, bordered by a crisp `1px solid #E7DEC8` perimeter, backed by a subtle tinted shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(31, 22, 18, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Elements & Order Slips):** The sticky order review docket and floating customization trays use an espresso-tinted drop shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(31, 22, 18, 0.10)`, trimmed with double-line top borders or jagged receipt perforation edges.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Customizer Drawers):** High-prominence overlays featuring a warm dark backdrop scrim (`rgba(31, 22, 18, 0.6)`) and sharp, tactile cards elevated with `0 16px 40px rgba(31, 22, 18, 0.18)`.

## Shapes
A soft, structured roundedness scale (`roundedness: 1`) reflects physical bakery cardboard boxes, embossed tin ceiling panels, and traditional menu cards:
- Base elements (buttons, text fields, chips) use `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px).
- Cards, modal containers, and order drawers use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- Pills and circles are strictly reserved for quantity steppers, status dots, and circular pizza size badges (`12"`, `16"`, `18"`).
This avoids overly rounded modern tech aesthetics, retaining the authentic, handcrafted punch of a mid-century Italian-American establishment.
